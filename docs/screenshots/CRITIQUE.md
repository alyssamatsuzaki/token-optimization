# Screenshot critique

Two passes over Playwright screenshots, against `docs/DESIGN.md`. Screenshots are regenerated by
`make verify` (the e2e suite writes them), so this file describes what the pictures showed and
what changed because of it.

## Pass 1 — after Optimize (M5)

Six fixes, recorded in `DECISIONS.md` D21. The one that mattered:

**Findings were all styled as projected, so a measured finding looked like a guess.** W01 is
computed from 200 real calls; PL14 is a heuristic worth nothing. Rendering both in muted grey
with a dotted underline told the reader they were equally soft. Now each finding is styled by its
own confidence, so the two most valuable findings on the screen — both `measured` — read as ink
and weight while the heuristics stay grey.

The rest: a graph node's "in / out" row wrapped into a mangled label; the test-data banner
repeated the manifest note in different words; zero-dollar quality findings printed "$0.00000",
which reads as a measurement of zero rather than as "this one isn't about tokens"; and the
candidate graph was cropped at four nodes.

## Pass 2 — after the remaining screens (M6)

**Inspect opened on a dead end.** The screen loads B0's prompt by default and then told the
reader to "paste a prompt and press Inspect". It now inspects on load, so it opens on findings
rather than on an instruction to do what it already did.

**Provenance marks were on things that are not measurements.** Compare showed "Models compared:
3 ≈" and "Cheapest: Claude Haiku 4.5 ~". A count of the columns on screen is not an estimate of
anything, and a model *name* is not simulated — its cost is. `Figure` gained `kind="none"`, and
the marks moved onto the figures they actually describe. Marking things that are not
measurements devalues the marks on the things that are, which is the whole point of section 3 of
the design brief.

## The decorative element removed from each screen

`docs/DESIGN.md` closes by asking for one decorative element to come off every screen before
shipping. What went:

| Screen | Removed | Why it was decoration |
|---|---|---|
| Optimize | The cost and cost-share rows on the graph's "Prompt" node | It has no spend of its own. Printing `$0.00000` and `0%` filled the card out and said nothing. |
| Inspect | The "paste a prompt and press Inspect" placeholder panel | It occupied half the screen to describe a state that no longer exists. |
| Compare | The `≈` mark on the model count and the `~` on the two "cheapest / fastest" model names | Three marks that decorated a name instead of describing a measurement. |
| Spend | The separate "Mode" tile's redundant second line | The recording state is already stated in full on Settings; the tile now carries the recording date, which is the fact a reader needs here. |
| Settings | Nothing removed — the screen is a table of provenance and there was no ornament on it | Recorded here rather than inventing a removal for symmetry. |

## Still true after both passes

- No card grid. Sections are separated by hairline rules; `chalk` surfaces appear twice at most
  per screen and mean "this floats above the plane" (the frontier plot field, the trace drawer).
- One bold element: the cost-quality frontier. Everything around it is grey, black and hairlines.
- One orchestrated motion: the baseline→candidate graph morph.
- Every number carries its unit, and every quality claim carries an interval and an n.
